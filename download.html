How to Install on Android
1
Open the App in Chrome
Tap the button at the bottom of this page to open the Break Free Tribe app. Make sure you are using the Chrome browser.
📱 Use Google Chrome for best results
2
Tap the Three Dots Menu
When the app opens, tap the three dots ⋮ at the top right corner of your Chrome browser.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
In the menu that appears, scroll down and tap "Add to Home screen". A popup will appear asking you to confirm.
✓ The app icon will appear on your home screen
4
Tap "Add" to Confirm
Tap Add on the popup. The Break Free Tribe app will now appear on your phone home screen just like any other app.
5
Open and Enjoy!
Tap the Break Free Tribe icon on your home screen. The app opens fullscreen with no browser bar, just like a real app. Glory to God!
🎉 You now have the app on your phone for free!
How to Install on iPhone
1
Open in Safari
Open the Break Free Tribe app using the Safari browser on your iPhone. It must be Safari, not Chrome.
2
Tap the Share Button
Tap the share icon at the bottom of Safari. It looks like a box with an arrow pointing up ⇧
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll through the share options and tap "Add to Home Screen". Confirm by tapping Add at the top right.
✓ The app will appear on your iPhone home screen
